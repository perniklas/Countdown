# Theme redesign references

The Clair Obscur direction draws on the official game's painterly fantasy world and Sandfall's art direction: [expedition33.com](https://www.expedition33.com/) and [sandfall.co](https://www.sandfall.co/). The implementation uses a misty Belle Epoque ruin, a weathered 33 monolith, and crimson petals, with a quieter left side reserved for the countdown.

The Technical Metal direction draws on industrial cover art and dense technical linework: [The Zenith Passage on Metal Blade](https://www.metalblade.com/thezenithpassage/), [Archspire's *Bleed the Future*](https://archspire.bandcamp.com/album/bleed-the-future), and the [Acid Noir music-site concept](https://dribbble.com/shots/26890627-tradebox-Acid-Noir-music-website). The implementation uses a biomechanical alloy lattice, charcoal, bone, and restrained furnace light, with a dark left side for text.

Both background artworks were generated originally with the built-in ImageGen tool for this project. They do not reproduce or reuse game, band, or album artwork. The WebP assets in the workspace are compressed versions of those generated images.

## Generated asset prompts

Both assets were made with the built-in ImageGen tool. The generated PNGs were compressed into the workspace WebP files listed below.

- **Clair Obscur** (`public/themes/clair-obscur-world.webp`): Cinematic wide French Belle Epoque ruined architecture, painterly mist, a colossal weathered monolith marked 33 in the right third, crimson petals, aged ivory, slate, umber, and muted gold. Keep the left 60% quiet for text.
- **Technical Metal** (`public/themes/technical-metal-world.webp`): Original extreme technical-metal album art with asymmetric blackened titanium vertebrae, dense fractal lattices, and serrated cables concentrated on the right 45%; a faint burnt-amber cavity; bone porcelain edges; charcoal, oxidized iron, and copper. Keep the left 55% dark and quiet for text. No logos, interface elements, or cyan grid.
